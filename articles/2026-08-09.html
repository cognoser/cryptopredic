Market context

Bitcoin is around $64,783 with a -0.22% 24-hour move, while Ether is around $1,916.45 with a +0.14% move. Those figures are a snapshot, not a forecast. The useful question is whether the move is supported by spot demand, volume, and a clear change in the news.
